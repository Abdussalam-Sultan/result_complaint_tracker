3. STEP-BY-STEP DEMO PRESENTATION SCENARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCENARIO 1: MASS COMPLAINTS CLUSTERING & BULK RESOLUTION
  1. Log in as Lecturer 1:
     - Email: lecturer1@oauife.edu.ng
     - Password: password123
  2. Notice the top section: "Mass Complaint Clusters (Grouped Issues)".
     - There is an active card for "CSC 301: Missing Score Issue Cluster"
       affecting 25 students.
  3. Click "Review Group":
     - View the list of all 25 affected students and their matric numbers.
     - Select "Bulk Resolve".
     - Enter an audit note: "CA score sheets retrieved from department vaults. All 25 students updated to awarded CA marks."
     - Click "Execute Bulk RESOLVE".
  4. Result:
     - The group is marked RESOLVED.
     - An individual StatusEvent is atomically written to all 25 students'
       private timelines with the lecturer's explanation.
     - All 25 students receive in-app notifications.

SCENARIO 2: ISOLATING A SINGLE STUDENT FROM A MASS CLUSTER
  1. If a student's situation is unique within a mass issue, open any cluster
     modal as a lecturer.
  2. Find a specific student in the table and click "Remove from Group".
  3. The student's complaint item is detached from the bulk cluster and
     becomes an individual complaint item that can be reviewed independently.

SCENARIO 3: MULTI-ISSUE COMPLAINT (INDEPENDENT LIFECYCLES)
  1. Log in as Student Ayomide Balogun:
     - Email: ayomide@oauife.edu.ng
     - Password: password123
  2. Look at Ayomide's CSC 303 complaint:
     - Click on the complaint card to open the detail view.
     - Item 1 (Missing CA) is in "UNDER REVIEW".
     - Item 2 (Wrong Letter Grade) is in "SUBMITTED".
     - Notice that resolving or changing Item 1 does NOT close Item 2.
     - Overall status is dynamically derived: "IN PROGRESS (1 of 2 resolved)".
     - Every transition has a clear chronological timeline with actor name and note.

SCENARIO 4: DISPUTING A REJECTION & HOD ESCALATION (1-REOPEN LIMIT)
  1. Log in as Student Victor Ekanem:
     - Email: victor@oauife.edu.ng
     - Password: password123
  2. Open Victor's CSC 401 Script Remark complaint.
  3. Inspect the audit timeline:
     - Student submitted remark request.
     - Lecturer Dr. Okoye reviewed and rejected it with reason: "No marking discrepancies found".
     - Victor disputed the rejection: "Question 3 sub-part was not graded on script".
     - Because of the dispute, it automatically escalated to the HOD.
     - The policy badge now shows "Reopen limit reached (1 of 1 dispute allowed)".
  4. Now log in as the HOD:
     - Email: hod@oauife.edu.ng
     - Password: password123
  5. In the HOD dashboard, see Victor's dispute in the "Escalated Complaints Queue".
  6. Click "Decide Escalation":
     - Choose "Approve & Resolve" or "Final Rejection".
     - Enter the official HOD board minute note.
     - Click "Enforce HOD Decision".
     - Victor's status updates, and a notification is dispatched to his portal.

SCENARIO 5: UNASSIGNED COURSE AUTOMATIC HOD ROUTING
  1. Log in as HOD (hod@oauife.edu.ng).
  2. Notice the yellow alert banner: "Unassigned Courses Alert".
     - Course CSC 415 has no assigned lecturer.
  3. In the Escalated Queue, observe the complaint by Grace Anyanwu (CSC/2021/027):
     - The complaint bypassed the lecturer stage entirely and was routed
       directly to the HOD queue because the course is unassigned.

SCENARIO 6: LOGGING A NEW COMPLAINT AS A STUDENT
  1. Log in as any student (e.g. seun@oauife.edu.ng).
  2. Click "+ Log Result Complaint".
  3. Select an enrolled course from the dropdown (only registered courses appear).
  4. Tick one or more issue types (e.g. "Missing Score", "Wrong Letter Grade").
  5. Fill in optional claimed scores (0-100) and description.
  6. Click "Submit Result Complaint".
  7. Notice that duplicate prevention will block submitting the same issue on
     the same course while an open one exists.

SCENARIO 7: SLA COMPLIANCE & AUTO-ESCALATION CHECK (ADMIN)
  1. Log in as Administrator:
     - Email: admin@oauife.edu.ng
     - Password: password123
  2. Navigate to the "SLA Policy Engine" tab.
  3. Click "Run SLA Policy Check".
  4. Watch the engine evaluate open complaints:
     - Inactive items under lecturer review (> 7 days) are automatically escalated to the HOD.
     - Items pending student information (> 7 days) with no reply are auto-closed.
     - A full audit summary report is displayed.

4. HOW TO RESET / RE-SEED POSTGRESQL DATABASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application runs on a real SQL PostgreSQL database. To restore all complaints,
users, enrollments, and demonstration scenarios to their clean initial state at any time:
  npm run seed

Database Configuration:
  - Cross-platform embedded PostgreSQL with single-file snapshot persistence (database.tar.gz).
    Works seamlessly on Windows, OneDrive folders, Mac, and Linux without file-locking issues.
  - Or connect to an external PostgreSQL server by defining DATABASE_URL or
    (PGHOST, PGUSER, PGPASSWORD, PGDATABASE) in the environment.
